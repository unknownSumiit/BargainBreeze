How Senior Discounts Work

  1. Check eligibility: Age requirements vary by store. Some start at 50 (with AARP), others at 55, 60, 62, or 65.
  2. Bring valid ID: Always carry a driver's license or state ID to verify your age when shopping in person.
  3. Ask if it applies: Cashiers won't always ask. It never hurts to politely ask, "Do you offer a senior discount?"
  4. Check exclusions: Many discounts do not apply to alcohol, gift cards, or clearance items.
  5. Confirm the offer: Policies can change rapidly, and franchise locations may not participate in national offers.

Important Disclaimer

Discount availability, age requirements, exclusions, and participating locations can change at any time without notice. BargainBreeze provides these listings for informational purposes as a guide.
